#### 2. Error Context Analysis:
- **Contextual Conditions:**
  - The task involves a practical application of scientific principles (e.g., buoyancy, stability).
  - The question presents multiple options, each of which requires a different level of technical understanding.
  - The user is seeking a definitive answer to ensure the success of a project or experiment.
  - The task is complex and requires a step-by-step approach to fully understand and address.

- **Sequence of Interactions:**
  - The user provides a clear task with specific options.
  - The agent performs an initial search and gathers some relevant information.
  - The agent quickly decides on an option based on the first piece of information found.
  - The agent fails to perform a deeper search or analysis to confirm the chosen option.
  - The agent does not systematically evaluate all provided options.

#### 3. Detection Heuristics:
- **Specific Questions:**
  - "Have you considered all the options presented in the question?"
  - "What specific technical details did you find that support your chosen option?"
  - "Can you explain how each option relates to the principles of buoyancy and stability?"
  - "What additional information do you think is necessary to make a more informed decision?"

- **Analytical Framework:**
  - **Option Analysis:** Evaluate whether the agent has broken down each option and provided a rationale for why it might or might not be effective.
  - **Technical Depth:** Check if the agent's responses include specific technical details or principles relevant to the task (e.g., center of gravity, weight distribution).
  - **Systematic Approach:** Determine if the agent has followed a structured sequence of steps to gather and analyze information (e.g., initial search, detailed follow-up, option comparison).
  - **Confirmation Bias:** Identify if the agent has shown a tendency to favor the first piece of relevant information found without seeking additional confirmation.

- **Key Phrases or Conversation Patterns:**
  - "From the information gathered, it's clear that..."
  - "However, the focus on... lends more support to why..."
  - "The search indicated that... but the specific details were not fully visible."
  - "Based on this, the best way is option..."
  - "The guidance on... reinforces the necessity of option..."
  - "I think we should choose option... because..."
  - "Without further information, it seems that..."
  - "Let's go with option... since..."
